# Electricity Maps 2021 series (not redistributable)

Obtain academic access at electricitymaps.com and download the 2021 hourly CSVs for: AE, AU-NSW, BR-CS, FR, IN-WE, KR, SG, US-MIDA-PJM, ZA. Use the life-cycle (LCA) intensity column, gCO2e/kWh. Window used in the paper: 2021-06-01 00:00-04:00 UTC. Convert each zone to the `timestamp,gco2_per_kwh` format of the neighboring synthetic CSVs and place them under `data/carbon/em2021/`. Check against `SHA256SUMS-em2021.txt`.
